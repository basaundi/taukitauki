TaukiTauki

Privacy Policy

Short version: TaukiTauki does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data you type. Everything stays on your device.

What is TaukiTauki?

TaukiTauki is a flick-input keyboard for Android designed for the Basque language. It provides word predictions using a local dictionary stored on your device.

Data we do not collect

TaukiTauki does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No keystrokes or typed text are recorded or transmitted.
  • No usage statistics, analytics, or crash reports are sent anywhere.
  • No identifiers (device ID, IP address, advertising ID) are collected.
  • No account or registration is required.

How word prediction works

All word prediction and bigram suggestions are computed entirely on your device using a local SQLite database bundled with the app. This database never leaves your device and no network connection is made to perform predictions.

Permissions

TaukiTauki requests only the permissions required to function as a keyboard:

  • Input method (BIND_INPUT_METHOD): required for any Android keyboard to receive and process key events.

No internet, storage, location, camera, or microphone permissions are requested.

Third-party services

TaukiTauki does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, or cloud APIs. There are no third parties involved in the operation of this app.

Children's privacy

TaukiTauki does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for users of all ages.

Open source

The full source code of TaukiTauki is available at github.com/basaundi/taukitauki. You are welcome to inspect it to verify these claims.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the effective date below will be updated. Given the nature of the app (no data collection), material changes are not anticipated.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project's GitHub repository via an issue or discussion.
